Have you ever had burning/tingling pain or electric shooting pain into the toes? One cause for this could be Morton's Neuroma. A Morton's Neuroma is an accumulation of scar tissue around the nerve bundle between the 3rd and 4th interdigital space of your toes. Located below the deep transverse ligament, this nerve bundle. Common causes include overuse, tight calves, poor footwear, and increased activity.
